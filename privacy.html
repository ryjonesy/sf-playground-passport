Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

SF Playground Passport is a free, non-commercial family project for tracking playground visits.

What we collect

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your email address and a unique account ID. We store your playground progress (visited, rating, notes, dates, trip plan) so it syncs across your devices. We do not receive your Google password.

How it is used

Your data is used only to save and restore your progress. We do not sell, share, or use it for advertising. Each account can access only its own data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase. Without signing in, progress stays only in your browser.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and data, open an issue at github.com/ryjonesy/sf-playground-passport/issues.
